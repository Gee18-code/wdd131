Website Planning Document

Site Name

GEE Autos

GEE Autos is a vehicle discovery and inquiry website that helps customers explore quality vehicles and learn more about their features and specifications.

I chose the name GEE Autos because it is simple, memorable, and represents a modern automotive brand.

Site Purpose

The purpose of GEE Autos is to provide visitors with an easy way to browse vehicles, compare important vehicle information, and submit inquiries about vehicles they are interested in.

The website will provide vehicle listings, specifications, pricing information, featured vehicles, and a contact form for customers who want to make an inquiry.

Scenarios

  • Which vehicles are currently available and what are their prices?
  • What are the features and specifications of a particular vehicle?
  • How can i contact GEE Autos to make an inquiry about a vehicle?
  • How can i know what to budget a particular vehicle?

Color Schema

The GEE Autos color scheme uses black, cream, and gold to create a clean, modern, and premium automotive appearance.

  • Black (#111111): Used for headings, navigation, and important text.
  • Cream (#F5F1E8): Used as the primary page background.
  • Gold (#C9A227): Used for accents, buttons, borders, and highlights.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Used for cards and text placed on dark backgrounds.

GEE Autos will use two primary fonts to create a modern and very reliable style.

  • Montserrat: Used for headings, navigation, buttons, and important titles.
  • open Sans: Used for paragraphs, descriptions, forms, and general body content.
