Site Name

MedShelf Lagos

"Med" signals medical supplies and "Shelf" suggests an organized, browsable catalog, like a well-stocked storeroom. "Lagos" tells visitors the service is local to the city.

Optional domain availability: medshelf-lagos.com

Site Purpose

The site gives clinics, pharmacies, and small hospitals in Lagos one place to browse medical supplies, check what is in stock, and request a quote. It provides:

  • A product catalog grouped by category, such as gloves, syringes, and diagnostic kits
  • Stock availability and delivery information for Lagos areas
  • A quote request form for bulk orders
  • Contact details for the procurement team

Scenarios

Questions a visitor from the target audience would ask:

  • Which brands of examination gloves are in stock, and how much do they cost per box?
  • How do I request a bulk quote for my clinic and how soon will I get a reply?
  • Do you deliver to the Lagos Mainland and the Island, and how long does it take?
  • Where can I find the phone number or email of the procurement team?

Typography

This page uses the same fonts as the planned site.

  • Poppins (600 and 700): page titles, headings, navigation, and buttons.
  • Open Sans (400 and 600): paragraphs, product descriptions, tables, and form labels.

Both fonts load from Google Fonts. Fallbacks are Arial and sans-serif so text stays readable if the fonts fail to load.
